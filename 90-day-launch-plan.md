# Reengineering CoE: First 90 Days

**Proposed launch plan | Timing depends on access, capacity and approved scope**

| Period | My focus | Tangible output | Decision required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Days 1–30** | Listen to business and local leaders; examine complaints, rework, backlog and financial data; assess existing improvement capability | Agreed charter, named owners, baseline definitions, capability/capacity assessment and initial problem inventory | Sponsor confirms decision rights and priorities |
| **Days 31–60** | Map selected value streams; validate root causes; compare process, policy, training and automation options | Prioritized roadmap, one or two scoped pilot business cases, future-state designs and control plans | Business, Finance and technology approve scope, measures and resources |
| **Days 61–90** | Launch a feasible pilot; coach local teams; establish reporting and capture reusable patterns | Pilot evidence, adoption/exception dashboard, initial playbook and scale/stop recommendation | Sponsor decides next investment based on evidence |

## What I would examine in insurance distribution

Quote-to-purchase drop-off, service handoffs, repeated data entry, renewal follow-up, complaint resolution and payment/commission reconciliation are candidate discovery areas. These are hypotheses to test with company data, not findings about a specific organization.

Compare acquisition-related variation with issues already present in existing operations. Prioritize by business impact and controllability, rather than assuming every acquired process should immediately be replaced.

## What success looks like

By day 90, leaders share a fact-based view of the priority problems, the CoE has clear ownership and committed capacity, and at least one feasible pilot is underway or evaluated. The six-month agenda should demonstrate measured outcomes and a repeatable operating rhythm; it should not depend on declaring an entire enterprise transformed in 90 days.
