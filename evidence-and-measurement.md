## Benefit discipline

Keep separate fields for approved baseline, target, forecast, observed result and Finance-accepted realized benefit. Record period, volume, denominator, owner, calculation and evidence location. Separate cash reduction from capacity released, cost avoidance, revenue contribution and customer/control outcomes.

The experience summaries do not reproduce employer source records. Measurement methods, dashboards and business cases labeled **synthetic** or **illustrative** are portfolio demonstrations, not historical evidence for those experience claims.

## Enterprise and investment scale

My experience includes Wells Fargo, USAA and Booz Allen Hamilton. Asset values, the reach of an organization and a program's projected benefit are different from a transformation budget personally owned. Investment ownership should be described using the actual approved budget and decision authority.
