# Transformation Intake and Prioritization

**Reusable template | Scores support decisions; they do not replace judgment**

## Minimum intake

Capture the process and owner, affected customer, volume, current delay/rework, financial exposure, locations, data sources, existing controls, dependencies and the decision requested. Ask for the problem and baseline before asking for a preferred technology.

## Triage before scoring

Route urgent incidents and material control concerns through their established response paths. Combine duplicate requests, confirm scope and ownership, and separate evidence from assumptions. Mark low-confidence estimates for discovery rather than giving them false precision.

## Proposed weighted score

Score each factor from 0 to 5 using documented evidence. Weighted score = sum(weight × rating / 5), out of 100.

| Factor | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Financial / capacity value | 30 | Baseline, unit cost, benefit class and realization assumptions |
| Customer impact | 25 | Delay, abandonment, complaints or service-quality evidence |
| Control / quality improvement | 20 | Defect frequency, severity, control coverage and exceptions |
| Data and operational readiness | 15 | Data quality, owner commitment and ability to test |
| Delivery feasibility | 10 | Dependencies, capacity and effort; a higher score means easier delivery |

Do not let a high return score override a mandatory control action. Review high-impact, low-readiness ideas as discovery candidates; select quick wins only when they also solve a meaningful problem.

## Record the decision

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Disposition | Discover, pilot, deliver, defer, combine or reject |
| Why now | Outcome, urgency, trade-off and alternatives |
| Commitment | Accountable owner, resources and next gate |
| Financial basis | Cash, capacity, cost avoidance or revenue contribution; no double counting |
| Follow-up | Decision date, reviewer, evidence location and next review |

The [enterprise AI enablement case](https://github.com/ddjfam/agentic-ai-automation-playbook/blob/main/enterprise-ai-enablement.md) describes my experience establishing intake and screening opportunities. This scoring model is a reusable portfolio proposal, not a claim that these exact weights were used in that engagement.
