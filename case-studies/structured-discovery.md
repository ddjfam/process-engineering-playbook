# Booz Allen Hamilton: Structured Discovery to Implementation

**Experience summary | Senior Organizational Strategy & Business Process Engineer | 2017–2019**

**Reported result:** 62 process gaps closed across six client initiatives; 18 process maps updated.

## Business problem

A defense client's certification process involved multiple agencies and stakeholders. Previous improvement efforts had not resolved delays. Leaders needed an agreed view of the problems and a practical implementation plan.

## My contribution

I reviewed policies, prior improvement attempts and process data before facilitating **18 sessions with 12 subject-matter experts**. I mapped the current-state handoffs, examined root causes, identified waste and control gaps, and translated the findings into future-state processes and implementation priorities.

My role was to move the work from competing views of the problem to decisions that teams could execute. I used structured facilitation and evidence to connect process findings to scope, risk areas and priorities.

## Result and boundary

The work closed **62 gaps across six initiatives** and updated **18 process maps**. These are operational improvement outputs and reported outcomes; no additional cost saving or cycle-time percentage is inferred here.

## How I apply this today

I start with a defined customer outcome, map actual work, separate waiting from processing time, and test root-cause hypotheses before proposing automation. The [structured discovery guide](../improvement-frameworks.md) shows the reusable method, including handoff ownership, decision points and control design.
