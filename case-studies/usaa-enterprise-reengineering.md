# USAA: Enterprise Process Reengineering

**Experience summary | Senior Process Engineer, contractor | 2019–2020**

**Reported result:** 35% productivity improvement through redesign of 25 IT asset processes with 600+ steps; the targeted compliance issue was resolved across 33 regions.

## Business problem and scale

USAA's “Be a Compliant Company” effort required consistent, scalable IT asset processes. The engagement covered approximately **134,000 assets valued at $227M**, with work spanning business, technology, legal and risk partners. Leaders had different views on the approach and needed a common framework.

The $227M figure describes the assets in scope. It is not a project budget, investment managed or savings claim.

## My accountability and decisions

I led the process-engineering work needed to align leaders on an end-to-end approach with requirements and controls built into the design.

- Facilitated **15 cross-functional sessions**, compared approaches and helped leaders agree on a common framework.
- Mapped **25 processes and 600+ steps**, linking regulatory requirements to process artifacts.
- Aligned owners on requirements, controls, acceptance criteria and performance measures.
- Worked with technology partners on the future state and supported adoption through coaching.

The leadership turning point was agreement on a shared approach: individual process preferences had to be evaluated against the requirements and outcomes of the whole process.

## Outcome and measurement

The engagement reported **35% higher productivity** and resolution of the targeted issue across **33 regions**. The public summary reports the engagement outcome; detailed source measurements are not reproduced here.

## Reusable approach

For a new engagement, I would require a signed baseline, a clear productivity definition, comparable before/after periods and a named benefits owner. The [measurement guide](../metrics-and-operational-performance.md) and [change plan](../implementation-and-change-planning.md) show that discipline.

This case demonstrates enterprise alignment, controls by design and multi-location implementation. The linked templates are recreated portfolio tools rather than original USAA documents.
